LIFECYCLE STAGES - EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD CONNECTIONS

1. OPPORTUNITY
   - Gate: PG0 (entry point; 25% credit begins)
   - The initial identification of a value proposition
   - Where the executive goal/target gets set once
   - Connection: The Target number on the executive dashboard originates here
   - This is the ambition — the goal they're trying to reach
   - Set once and stays fixed throughout the lifecycle

2. VALUE CASE
   - Gate: PG0 to PG3 (MAPV uses rising gate factor as it matures)
   - Proves what the portfolio can support
   - MAPV (Maturity-Adjusted Portfolio Value) is calculated here
   - MAPV = Validated Delivered + (Gate factor × eligible remaining value)
   - Connection: MAPV on executive dashboard originates here
   - Challenged/unsupported remaining value counts as zero until re-assured

3. GATES PG0–PG5
   - Gate: PG0, PG1, PG2, PG3 (the pre-commitment checkpoints themselves)
   - Decision points throughout the lifecycle
   - PG0 starts at 25%; gates freeze commitments and gate history
   - These numbers never change once set (immutable)
   - Connection: Gate status affects MAPV and Forecast on dashboard
   - PG4 is where Original Commitment freezes

4. COMMITMENT
   - Gate: PG4 (passing this gate IS the commitment event)
   - The Original Commitment freezes at PG4
   - This is the frozen number executives compare against
   - Connection: Executives see "we committed $X, what have we actually delivered?"
   - Never rewritten — history is preserved

5. DELIVERY
   - Gate: PG4 to PG5 (work runs while the initiative keeps maturing)
   - The actual work of producing value
   - Feeds the Delivered number on the dashboard
   - Connection: As work progresses, Validated and Banked values rise
   - The gap between Forecast and Delivered is the executive's key visibility point

   DEFINITION — "Delivery" (this stage):
   The actual, ongoing work of producing the promised value — Owner-
   reported, mutable, changes daily. This is raw progress only: doing the
   work and reporting status. By itself, Stage 5 proves nothing and counts
   for nothing on the dashboard yet — it becomes proof only one stage
   later, at Validation (Stage 6), when Finance checks and signs off on a
   tranche. See Stage 6 for how the dashboard number "Delivered" is
   actually built from this stage's raw work.

6. VALIDATED
   - Gate: PG5 (Finance validates near full maturity)
   - Finance validates specific tranches of work
   - Each validated tranche adds exactly to Delivered
   - Example: $80k gap "ages in a queue" until new evidence justifies more
   - Connection: Every validated tranche directly increases Delivered on dashboard
   - Tranches can never exceed the original claim (database constraint)

   DEFINITION — "Delivered" (the dashboard number):
   Built entirely from this stage, not from Stage 5. The dashboard figure
   called "Delivered" is shorthand for "Validated Delivered" (the exact
   term the MAPV formula uses) — the running total of every tranche of
   work Finance has validated here, and nothing more. A claim only adds to
   Delivered once Finance checks and signs off on it, never just because
   the Owner reported it done back in Stage 5. Ledger-derived, so it rises
   in exact increments: a $700k claim where Finance validates $620k adds
   exactly $620k to Delivered — the remaining $80k sits unconfirmed in a
   queue, not counted, until new evidence justifies another tranche.
   How it differs from the other three executive numbers:
     - Target: the original ambition, set once, never moves
     - Commitment: the frozen pledge at PG4, what was promised
     - Forecast: the Owner's daily prediction of what will happen
     - Delivered: the only one of the four that's proven fact, not a
       prediction or a pledge
   Delivered is not the same as Banked: Banked is a further-locked,
   CFO-certified subset of what's already Delivered. Banked value does
   not add to Delivered again — it's already counted; Banked just marks
   that portion as final and audited.

7. BANKED
   - Gate: PG5 (CFO certifies and banking happens at the final gate)
   - Finance-validated values that are CFO-certified
   - Above materiality threshold, certified for banking
   - Connection: This is the "proven fact" number on dashboard — the only one that's real, not a prediction
   - The person who validates a value can never also bank it (segregation of duties)
   - CFO can delegate banking up to a ceiling (e.g., $2m); above that stays with CFO

   DEFINITION — "Banked" is two NEW actions on top of an already-validated
   amount, not a re-check of it. Validation (Stage 6) is finished and
   closed before Banked even starts:

     Stage 6 (VALIDATED) — already done, produces "Delivered"
       Finance validates -> confirms evidence, amount is accurate
     ---------------- Stage 6 ends. Stage 7 begins. ----------------
     Stage 7 (BANKED) — two new actions on the already-validated amount
       1. Certification -> CFO (or delegate) materiality/governance sign-off
       2. Banking Authority locks it -> ledger-final, immutable

   Banked does not add new value to Delivered — that amount was already
   counted at validation. Banking just takes a (possibly smaller) subset
   of the already-Delivered amount and marks that portion certified and
   permanently locked.

8. EMBED
   - Gate: PG5 complete (value goes operational after the final gate)
   - System integrates delivered value into client operations
   - Connects to "One app, two threads" principle
   - Value embeds across web/mobile/SAP platforms
   - Connection: The delivered value becomes operational reality for the executive's organization

9. SUSTAIN
   - Gate: PG5 complete (checked against the original Target post-gate)
   - Long-term viability of delivered value
   - Executives see whether value sustains the original Target/MAPV goal
   - Connection: Persistent Value-at-Risk gap assessment — does delivered value sustain the original ambition?
   - Key question: Is the organization better off long-term having gone through this lifecycle?

EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD SUMMARY (4 Numbers):
- Target: From Opportunity — the goal
- MAPV: From Value Case + Gates — maturity-weighted estimate
- Forecast: From Owner's prediction — what will happen
- Delivered: From Validated + Banked — what has been proven

Each stage leaves an immutable trace in the audit log that the API reads to render the four-number dashboard. The lifecycle trail ensures every dashboard number can be traced back to its originating stage.
