SIGNAL GATES AND LIFECYCLE CONNECTIONS
======================================

GATE SYSTEM OVERVIEW
--------------------
There are 6 gate levels: PG0 through PG5.
Gates are checkpoints embedded in the 9-stage lifecycle spine.
They are NOT a separate loop that finishes before commitment.
Commitment happens AT PG4, not after PG5.

PG0 starts at 25% credit.
PG4 is where the Original Commitment freezes (immutable).
PG5 is the final maturity checkpoint.

STAGE-TO-GATE MAPPING
---------------------

Stage 1: OPPORTUNITY      --> PG0 (entry point; 25% credit begins)
Stage 2: VALUE CASE       --> PG0 to PG3 (MAPV uses rising gate factor)
Stage 3: GATES            --> PG0, PG1, PG2, PG3 (pre-commitment checkpoints)
Stage 4: COMMITMENT       --> PG4 (Original Commitment freezes here)
Stage 5: DELIVERY         --> PG4 to PG5 (work runs while maturing)
Stage 6: VALIDATED        --> PG5 (Finance validates near full maturity)
Stage 7: BANKED           --> PG5 (CFO certifies at final gate)
Stage 8: EMBED            --> PG5 complete (value goes operational)
Stage 9: SUSTAIN          --> PG5 complete (check vs original Target)

GATE EXPLANATIONS (simple sentence each)
----------------------------------------

PG0: The idea enters at the first gate with 25% credit.
PG1: Early gate; the value case starts to take shape.
PG2: Mid gate; the plan is de-risked.
PG3: Pre-commit gate; ready to lock the number.
PG4: Commitment gate; the Original Commitment freezes forever.
PG5: Final gate; full maturity before delivery completes.

DASHBOARD CONNECTIONS
---------------------
Target      <- Stage 1 Opportunity (the goal, set once)
MAPV        <- Stage 2 Value Case + Stage 3 Gate factor (maturity-weighted estimate)
Forecast    <- Owner's prediction (runs alongside lifecycle)
Delivered   <- Stage 6 Validated + Stage 7 Banked (the only proven fact)

KEY RULE
--------
You do not finish PG0 through PG5 before committing.
You walk the lifecycle stages and pass gates as you mature.
Commitment happens at PG4 (Stage 4), then Delivery continues and PG5 can still occur afterward.

WHO / WHAT TRIGGERS A GATE INCREASE
-----------------------------------
The artifact does not name a single role that clicks "advance gate."
What is known:

- Gates do NOT auto-slide forward as tasks are checked off.
- Web scope includes "Value Case/Gate submissions" and "Run Review" —
  so someone submits evidence that the initiative has matured.
- A review evaluates the evidence.
- If it passes, the gate moves up and locks.
- The authority policy engine records and enforces the gate decision:
  it checks role × scope × authority × materiality × delegation on
  every request.
- Once recorded, the gate decision and snapshot are immutable — the
  database rejects any UPDATE or DELETE on them.

Bottom line: a person (Owner / Finance / reviewer) submits evidence,
a review evaluates it, and the system locks the gate upward. The gate
moves by human decision backed by system enforcement, not by automatic
project progress.

COMMITMENT SUMMARY (Stage 4 / PG4)
----------------------------------
- Commitment is Stage 4 of the 9-stage lifecycle. It happens AT PG4,
  not after all gates are finished.
- At PG4, the Original Commitment freezes permanently. It can never
  be rewritten, updated, or deleted. Corrections are added as new
  linked records (e.g., Value Adjustment), never overwrites.
- It is the fixed benchmark executives compare against: "we committed
  $X, what have we actually delivered?"
- Commitment is separate from Target, Forecast, and Delivered:
    * Target = the ambition, set once at Stage 1 (e.g., 50).
    * Commitment = the frozen pledge at PG4 (e.g., 40).
    * Forecast = the Owner's prediction; moves daily.
    * Delivered = proven work rising toward Commitment.
- Delivered grows toward Commitment, NOT necessarily toward Target.
  The gap between Target and Commitment is the Value-at-Risk.
- Commitment is NOT one of the four CEO dashboard numbers
  (Target, MAPV, Forecast, Delivered). It operates behind the scenes
  as the immutable reference point.
- Once frozen, Commitment is immutable under the same rules as gate
  decisions: database-level enforcement, hash-chained audit log, no
  standing UPDATE/DELETE on immutable tables.

DELIVERY SUMMARY (Stage 5)
--------------------------
- Delivery is Stage 5 of the 9-stage lifecycle. It is the actual work
  of producing value, running after Commitment freezes at PG4.
- Delivery feeds the Delivered number on the executive dashboard.
- As work progresses, Validated and Banked values rise.
- Delivery spans PG4 through PG5 — work continues while the initiative
  matures to the final gate.
- The gap between Forecast and Delivered is the executive's key
  visibility point: Forecast is a prediction; Delivered is proven fact.
- Delivered does not equal Commitment. Commitment is the frozen pledge;
  Delivered is what has actually been proven so far.
- Delivery is mutable (progress changes daily) until it is validated
  (Stage 6) and banked (Stage 7), at which point it becomes immutable
  proof.

DELIVERY AND VALIDATION — WORKING TOGETHER
------------------------------------------
Yes — exactly right. Delivery produces the work; Validation confirms it.
Only what Finance validates actually counts as Delivered on the
dashboard. They work hand in hand: Delivery creates the value,
Validation makes it count.

- Stage 5 (Delivery): Work is actually done. Value is produced.
- Stage 6 (Validated): Finance reviews that work and confirms it's real.
  Only then does it count toward the Delivered number.
- Done but not validated: sits in a queue; not counted yet (e.g., the
  $80k gap ages until new evidence justifies another tranche).
- Validated: added exactly to Delivered. Tranches can never exceed the
  original claim (database constraint).

VALIDATED SUMMARY (Stage 6)
---------------------------
- Validated is Stage 6 of the 9-stage lifecycle. Finance validates
  specific tranches of work.
- Each validated tranche adds exactly to Delivered.
- Challenged or unsupported remaining value counts as zero until
  re-assured.
- Tranches can never exceed the original claim (database constraint).
- Validated work becomes the basis for Banked (Stage 7).

BANKED SUMMARY (Stage 7)
------------------------
- Banked is Stage 7 of the 9-stage lifecycle. It is Finance-validated
  value that is CFO-certified.
- Only validated values can be banked. Banked does NOT add to Delivered;
  Delivered already increased at validation. Banked locks the validated
  value as final, immutable proof.
- This is the "proven fact" number on the dashboard — the only one that
  is real, not a prediction.
- Segregation of duties: the person who validates a value can never
  also bank it. Finance validates; a different authority (CFO or
  delegated Finance Director) banks.
- DEFAULT: the CFO is the Banking Authority. By default the CFO does
  both — certifies the value, then also personally banks it. There is
  no separate third role in the common case.
- Ceiling rule: The CFO MAY delegate just the banking action (never
  certification) up to a ceiling (e.g., $2m) to a Finance Director.
  Anything at or above that ceiling always stays with the CFO — it
  never falls through to the delegate, no matter what.
- Once banked, the value is immutable. Corrections are added as new
  linked records (Banking Adjustment), never overwrites.

EMBED SUMMARY (Stage 8)
------------------------
- Embed is Stage 8 of the 9-stage lifecycle. The system integrates
  delivered value into the client's operations.
- Connects to "One app, two threads" — value embeds across web,
  mobile, and SAP platforms.
- The delivered value becomes operational reality for the executive's
  organization.

EMBED — EMPHASIS
----------------
Embed = taking the flow/method that produced the banked value and
making it part of everyday routine across web, mobile, and SAP — so
the positive result sustains instead of being a one-time win.

Example:
- Banked: $200k saved using better routes (proven, validated, CFO-certified).
- Embed: Drivers use the mobile app daily to log routes; managers
  watch route savings on web; SAP auto-updates fuel costs and route
  plans. The $200k saving becomes how the company operates, not just
  a dashboard number.

MAPV = Validated Delivered + (Eligible Remaining Value × Current Gate Factor)
MAPV equals your proven delivered value plus your remaining eligible value, weighted by how mature the initiative is at its current gate.

- Validated Delivered: The proven, Finance-confirmed value so far.
- Eligible remaining value × Gate factor: Not just any "future potential gain" — it's the remaining value that's still eligible, weighted by how mature/de-risked it is (PG0 starts at 25% credit; PG5 is near full credit).
- Challenged/unsupported remaining value counts as zero until it's re-assured.
